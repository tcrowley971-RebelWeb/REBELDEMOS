Good to know.

How far ahead should I book? +

For colour or bridal, two to three weeks ahead is ideal. Cuts and shorter treatments can often be booked within the week, and we post last-minute openings on Instagram.

Do I need a patch test? +

Yes, for any new colour client or if it's been more than six months. It takes five minutes and needs to be done 48 hours before your appointment. Just pop in.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Please give us 24 hours' notice so we can offer the slot to someone else. Late cancellations may be charged 50% of the treatment.

Do you sell gift vouchers? +

Yes, in any amount. Buy them in the studio or over the phone and we'll post or email them.

Is there parking? +

Pay-and-display parking on the street outside, and a multi-storey car park two minutes' walk away.
