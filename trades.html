Common questions.

Do you charge for call-out quotes?+
No — quotes are free and come with no obligation to book. For emergency callouts we'll give you a price over the phone before anyone travels out.
How quickly can you get to an emergency?+
Usually within the hour in the city, a little longer for outlying areas. We'll always give you an honest estimate when you call.
Are you fully insured and certified?+
Yes — fully insured, and RGI registered for all gas work. Certificates provided for any gas safety or boiler work on request.
Do you offer any guarantee on your work?+
All installation work is guaranteed for 12 months, parts and labour. Manufacturer warranties apply on top of that for new boilers and appliances.
