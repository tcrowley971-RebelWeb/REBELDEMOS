Our story

Why we roast small.

Foundry started as one roaster in a lock-up unit and a lot of early mornings. We've kept it small on purpose, because it's the only way to taste every batch before it goes out the door.

Today we roast twice a week, bake every morning, and still know most of our regulars by their order. Pull up a stool and stay a while.

Do you have oat or plant milk? +

Yes. Oat, almond, soy and coconut are all available on any milk-based drink.

Can I buy your beans to take home? +

Absolutely. Bags of whatever we're roasting that week are at the counter, ground or whole bean.

Is there somewhere to sit and work? +

A few tables inside with plugs nearby and free wifi. It gets busy mid-morning, so afternoons are quieter.

Are dogs welcome? +

Of course. Water bowl at the door and a treat jar behind the counter.
